# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our Standards

Examples of positive behavior: being respectful, giving constructive feedback, showing empathy.

Examples of unacceptable behavior: trolling, harassment, publishing others' private information.

## Enforcement

Report to the project maintainer. All complaints will be reviewed promptly and fairly.

Adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
